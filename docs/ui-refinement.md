# Waymark — possibility becomes direction

## Approved direction

26 October 2026: evolve the existing Waymark identity. Retain the forest/porcelain
palette, editorial sans/serif pairing, original satin infinity and real planning
engine. Sir's signature requirement: **the same infinity becomes an aeroplane in
the second section, not a circular orbit or a separate plane icon.**

“Awwwards-level” is a quality ambition, not an award, acceptance or measured score.

27 October refinement: the aircraft is a **small traveller**, not another hero-sized
sculpture. After morphing, its center follows the actual second-section SVG line,
its nose follows the tangent, and scroll carries it through the boundary into the
sample-plan section. Reverse scroll retraces the route. Normal animated startup
must not paint the horizontal poster before revealing the slanted live infinity;
the poster remains only for reduced motion, unavailable WebGL and no JavaScript.

## Design decisions

- One object, one journey: an actual geometry morph carries the infinity from the
  hero into a 96px desktop / 64px mobile aircraft travelling on the route. Scroll remains native on touch. Reduced
  motion and denied WebGL retain readable content and static original artwork.
- A more intentional second chapter: “Less circling. More going.” The sculpture
  and its route support the message instead of competing with the text.
- Demonstrate before explaining: an editable, explicitly dated sample; fewer guide
  shortcuts in the marketing sample, with all guides retained in the planner.
- A usable editorial index: distinguish the introduction from the planner; expose
  the account entry without turning every navigation into a marketing sequence.
- Give the close a real headline and clear next action; replace outdated claims
  about guest persistence. Preview edits are temporary; provider-backed saving is
  unavailable until the dedicated account service is configured.
- Preserve keyboard operation, modal focus, section-deep-link behavior, honest
  states, sources and confidence distinctions. Fix the confirmed CSS-module defect
  rather than styling around its broken output.

## Boundaries and acceptance

No planning-date changes, invented testimonials/statistics, external product assets,
provider accounts, publishing, Git commits or deployment. The local showcase lab's
displayed performance numbers are not evidence for this work.

Check actual hero/intermediate/aircraft frames at desktop and touch sizes, forward
and reverse scroll, resize and live reduced-motion changes. Check no-JS/no-WebGL,
deep links, all planner screens, guide focus containment/restoration, account setup
layout and navigation. Run current type/lint/unit/build/browser gates; document
failures instead of replacing them with visual claims. Sir's visual approval is
still required even when technical checks pass.
